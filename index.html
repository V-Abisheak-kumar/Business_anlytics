Student Performance Analysis using Business Analytics

Which factors move a student’s results, and who needs help first? An interactive dashboard that turns academic records into decisions: describe, diagnose, predict and prescribe.

Explore the dashboard

The four stages of analytics

The dashboard below follows the standard business analytics ladder.

1 · Descriptive

What happened?

Averages, pass rate and grade distribution across departments.

2 · Diagnostic

Why did it happen?

Correlation of attendance, study hours, assignments and past GPA with scores.

3 · Predictive

What will happen?

A fitted trend line estimates the score gain from better attendance.

4 · Prescriptive

What should we do?

A ranked at-risk list with a suggested action for each student.
